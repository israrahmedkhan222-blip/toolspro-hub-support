    Your data stays on your phone

    The app has no accounts, no ads and no servers of its own. The developer can’t see what you enter, and deleting the app removes it.

    Stays on your device

    • Your entries. Loans, holdings, dates, Moments, saved cities and places, and settings are stored only on your phone.
    • Petal. Cycle dates, symptoms and notes you log never leave your phone. Petal gives estimates only. It isn’t medical advice and can’t be used to prevent pregnancy.
    • Motion sensors. Used on your device for the water-effect tiles, the level and climb tracking. Nothing is stored or shared.
    • Reminders. Scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.

    Uses the internet

    • Currency rates. The Currencies tool downloads public exchange rates. Requests contain only currency codes and dates. Like any internet request, the rate services can see your device’s IP address.
    • Place names. If you allow location, it’s used only while the app is open, and names are looked up with Apple’s built-in service. Your location isn’t stored on any server of ours.

    Do I need an account?

    No. There’s nothing to sign up for or sign in to. Open the app and use it.

    Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

    On your phone only. Delete the app and everything you entered goes with it. You can also remove single items inside each tool, for example by swiping a Moment.

    The exchange rates look out of date

    Rates come from public services and are shown with the date they were published, at the top of the Currencies tool. Tap the refresh icon while you’re online. Rates are for reference and aren’t what a bank or card will charge you.

    Compass or Timezones can’t find my location

    Location is optional. Open Settings on your iPhone, choose ToolsPro Hub, then Location, and allow it while using the app. You can still add cities and places by hand without it.

    Is Petal medical advice?

    No. Petal gives estimates from the dates you log. It can’t be used to prevent pregnancy. Please talk to a doctor about any health concern.

    Are the loan and portfolio numbers financial advice?

    No. They are calculators that show the maths of what you enter. Check real terms with your lender or broker before you decide.
