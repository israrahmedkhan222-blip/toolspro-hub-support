# ToolsPro Hub – Support

Welcome to the official support page for **ToolsPro Hub**.

ToolsPro Hub is an all-in-one app of everyday tools, designed to be clean, fast and privacy-friendly.

## 📱 App Features

- Loan Planner, with Penny, a built-in helper that explains each option
- Portfolio: stock profit and average calculators
- Timezones: world clock, converter and meeting planner
- Currencies: live exchange rates and charts
- Moments: dates, countdowns, milestones and age
- Petal: a private cycle and period tracker
- Compass: places, altitude, level and Qibla
- Units: converters, kitchen, prices and room estimates

## 🔒 Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

ToolsPro Hub respects your privacy. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data is collected or shared.

**What stays on your device.** Everything you enter, such as loans, holdings, dates and Moments, saved cities and places, settings, and everything you log in Petal, is stored only on your phone. The app has no servers of its own and the developer cannot see this data. It is not uploaded or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

**Petal.** Cycle dates, symptoms and notes you log in Petal stay on your device and are never uploaded. Petal gives estimates only. It is not medical advice and cannot be used to prevent pregnancy. Please talk to a doctor about any health concern.

**Currency rates.** The Currencies tool downloads public exchange rates from public rate services. These requests contain only currency codes and dates, never personal information. As with any internet request, those services can see your device's IP address.

**Location.** If you allow it, location is used only while the app is open: to detect your timezone and for Compass features such as saved places, altitude, sunrise and sunset, and Qibla. Place names are looked up with the built-in iOS service. Your location is not stored on any server of ours.

**Motion sensors.** Used on your device only, for the water-effect tiles, the level and climb tracking. Nothing is stored or shared.

**Notifications.** Reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device. The app does not use a server to send them.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
